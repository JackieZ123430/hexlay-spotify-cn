Pair Spotify with your car

Lets the Spotify app on your car's screen use your Spotify account

📷

VEHICLE SCAN REQUIRED

To ensure complete privacy and prevent mixing accounts across different cars, each BMW generates its own encrypted pairing QR code.

How to pair: 1. Open Spotify on your BMW iDrive screen.
2. Go to the Connect tab.
3. Point your smartphone camera at the displayed QR code.

Car paired on this phone before? ▾

The code only finds a car this phone already has: the car's key is only in its QR code, so a code someone glimpses on your screen is no use to them.

ACTIVE VEHICLE --------
🔒 E2EE Isolated Session: Encrypted exclusively for this vehicle.

You sign in on Spotify's own page (accounts.spotify.com). This page never sees your Spotify password; only an access token is sent to your car, encrypted with your car's key.

Only needed once: your browser and your car remember it. ✓ Saved

How do I get a Client ID? ▾
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify Premium account. Accept the developer terms if asked.
  2. Tap Create app. Any app name and description work, for example "My BMW".
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add exactly this address, then tap Add:
    https://hexlay.github.io/idrive7-custom-apps/pair.html
  4. Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?, tick Web API. Accept the terms and tap Save.
  5. On your new app's page, open Settings, copy the Client ID and paste it above. You don't need the Client secret.
  6. Tap Connect with Spotify below.

Every driver makes their own app. It's free and takes about two minutes, and nobody else can use your app without your approval. To let family members connect with the same app, add their Spotify email under Settings → User Management (Spotify allows up to 5 people per app).
