M-Copilot

Ask about your car on its screen: warning lights, service, tyres, fuel, performance and more. Answers use your car's live readings. Works with Gemini (free), Claude, ChatGPT and others.

To pair: on the car, open M-Copilot or Spotify and its Connect tab, then tap Scan car QR above.

Spotify

Your playlists and liked songs on the car's screen: play, skip, shuffle, and choose which device plays.

To pair: on the car, open Spotify and go to the Connect tab, then tap Scan car QR above.
