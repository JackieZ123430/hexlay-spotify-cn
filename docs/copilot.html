To ensure 100% privacy and prevent mixing data between different cars, each BMW generates a unique, isolated encryption QR code.

How to pair: 1. Open M-Copilot on your BMW iDrive screen.
2. Press Pair Phone on the bottom button bar (an already paired car stays paired).
3. Point your smartphone camera at the displayed QR code.

PHONE NOTIFICATIONS

Your car tells this phone about new warning lights and service coming due. The car sends them itself, encrypted for this phone only: no server of this project exists or sees them. The car has to be on and online to send.

2. Open iDrive Apps from the Home Screen.
3. Tap Scan car QR and scan the M-Copilot code on the car screen.
